How to Turn Your monday.com Project Board into a Monthly Retainer Report Your Clients Will Actually Read

📅 November 11, 2026 ✍️ agent7d Team ⏱ 5 min read

Retainer relationships are built on trust. But trust erodes quietly — usually around the 3rd or 4th month when a client looks at a recurring invoice and thinks, "What exactly are we paying for again?"

The work is real. The hours are logged. The tasks are done. But if your client only ever sees a charge hitting their card, you're leaving them to fill in the blanks themselves. That's a problem, because clients who fill in blanks tend to fill them in wrong.

A monthly retainer report fixes this. Not because clients are suspicious — but because visibility creates confidence, and confidence is what renews contracts.

---

The Problem

Most agencies and consultants running retainer engagements track everything inside monday.com: tasks completed, hours logged, status updates, deliverables shipped. The data is all there, organized and timestated.

But when it comes to sending the client something to look at, the process falls apart. Someone has to manually pull numbers from the board, write a summary in a Google Doc or Word file, format it to look professional, and send it — every single month, for every single client.

That's 45 minutes to two hours of non-billable work per client, per month. For a ten-client agency, you're looking at a full workday lost to administrative writing. And because it's tedious, it often gets deprioritized — which means clients go without updates, which means the invoice friction gets worse, not better.

The problem isn't that the information doesn't exist. It's that getting it out of monday.com and into a readable client document is entirely manual.

---

The Solution

The fix is to generate your monthly retainer report directly from your monday.com board — automatically, using the item data you're already capturing.

This is exactly what DocuAI does. It's a monday.com app that reads your board data and produces formatted, professional documents — reports, proposals, contracts — without any manual write-up. You set the template once, connect it to your board structure, and generate a polished client report in seconds whenever you need one.

For retainer reporting specifically, that means your completed tasks, hours used, deliverables delivered, and next month's focus can all be pulled into a professional document the moment you're ready to send it.

---

Step by Step

Here's how to build a repeatable monthly reporting workflow in monday.com using DocuAI:

1. Structure your board for reporting. Make sure your board captures the columns you want to report on: task name, status, assigned team member, hours logged, due date, and any deliverable links. If you're not tracking hours natively, add a Numbers column.

2. Use item tags or groups to separate monthly work. Create a group per month (e.g., "June 2025 — Client Name") or use a Date column to filter by period. This gives DocuAI a clean data set to pull from.

3. Set up your DocuAI template. Build a retainer report template that includes sections for: executive summary, tasks completed this month, hours used vs. hours included, key outcomes or wins, and priorities for next month. Map each section to the corresponding board columns.

4. Generate and review. At the end of the month, run DocuAI on the relevant items. The document populates automatically. Spend five minutes reviewing — not rewriting.

5. Send. Export as PDF or share directly. Done.
